Integrated M.S./Ph.D. Student, Computer Vision Lab, Ewha Womans University.

Computer Vision Lab

Ewha Womans University

Seoul, South Korea

I am an Integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at the Computer Vision Lab, Ewha Womans University, advised by Prof. Dongbo Min.

My research focuses on building models that understand real-world visual data, adapt across domains, and remain practical enough to deploy. My interests span computer vision, multimodal learning, domain-adaptive semantic segmentation, and self-supervised learning.

Feel free to reach out via email if you would like to discuss research or collaboration.
